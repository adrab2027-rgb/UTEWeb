Using Android phones

Android phones offer a wide range of features and customization options. They are known for their open-source nature, allowing users to modify and personalize their devices extensively.

How to use Android phones

Set up your Google account to access the Play Store and other Google services.

Customize your home screen with widgets and app shortcuts.

Explore the settings menu to adjust notifications, display, and security options.

Download apps from the Google Play Store to enhance functionality.

Use Google Assistant {Google Geminai} for voice commands and smart home integration.
